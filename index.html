VisionGuard Storage

A personal file-transfer connector for the VisionGuard AI research project. It lets the operator preserve selected research files in their own Google Drive and restore a working set on a device or compute server they choose.

هذه صفحة تعريف بوصلة تخزين شخصية. ملفات المشروع والأوزان والفيديوهات محفوظة في حساب المالك الخاص؛ هذه الصفحة لا تعرضها.

Access requested

The connector requests only drive.file: access to the specific Google Drive files used with this app. It is not designed to scan the user's entire Drive, email, contacts or browsing history.

How it works

Transfers use the Google Drive API, rclone and the operator's local tooling. Files move between the operator's Drive and the devices or compute providers they select. The connector is intended for the operator's own research workflow, not as a public file-sharing service.
