VisionGuard Storage privacy policy

الوصول يقتصر على ملفات Drive التي يستخدمها المالك مع التطبيق. لا تتضمن هذه الصفحة ملفات المشروع أو مفاتيح الدخول. ويمكن إلغاء صلاحية التطبيق من حساب Google.

Purpose and scope

VisionGuard Storage is a personal connector used by the project operator to back up and restore selected research files. Its Google permission is https://www.googleapis.com/auth/drive.file, covering only the specific files used with this app.

Data used

The connector transfers the content of the files the operator selects and reads the metadata needed to verify transfers, such as file names, sizes, identifiers and checksums. It may read the signed-in account identity and available storage quota to check the intended account and capacity. It does not request access to Gmail, contacts or unrelated Drive files.

Storage and processing

Files remain in the operator's Google Drive and on the devices or compute servers the operator chooses. Transfer manifests, logs and local download caches may be retained to verify integrity and resume interrupted work. Authorization credentials are stored separately from project code and are not included in public repositories or this website.

Sharing and use

The connector does not sell Google user data or use it for advertising. It transfers selected data to an external compute provider only as part of the operator's chosen workflow. The operator is responsible for choosing that provider, controlling access and obtaining appropriate permissions for the content. The storage connector itself does not train models or make monitoring decisions.

Retention and control

The operator controls the retention of Drive files, local copies, manifests, caches and results. Revoking the app's access does not automatically erase files already stored in Drive or copied to a device. Access can be revoked through Google Account connections. Deleting data requires a separate action by its owner.

Website hosting

No analytics or advertising scripts have been added to this information site. GitHub Pages hosts the pages and may process standard access information under GitHub's privacy statement.

Contact

Contact the operator through their GitHub profile. Do not post passwords, authorization tokens or private recordings in public messages.
